Can our crops survive the drought?

Build your knowledge

Four ideas that guide resilient farming

Open each idea before continuing. The language has been adapted for high-school learners from Chapter 13.

🌾

Agrobiodiversity

The variety of crops, crop varieties, genes, and other living things that support farming systems.

↻

Resilience

A farming system’s ability to resist, adapt, or transform while continuing to feed people.

🧬

Adaptive traits

Characteristics such as drought tolerance, early maturity, or seed dormancy that may help crops succeed under particular conditions.

Why does diversity matter?

Different crops and varieties respond differently to stress. Maintaining diversity gives farmers and breeders more possible responses when rainfall, heat, pests, or community needs change.

Does one “best crop” solve the problem?

Not necessarily. A crop can yield well but fail to meet food quality, market, cultural, or farmer-preference needs. Resilience requires attention to ecological and social conditions.

Source foundation: Thuillet et al., Chapter 13, pp. 331–360. Definitions are educational adaptations, not direct quotations.

Responsible AI statement

Generative AI assisted with drafting instructional wording, scenario prompts, and adaptive feedback. Every factual statement was checked against the cited OER chapters, and the final decisions were made by the designer. The embedded Crop Coach uses reviewed decision rules rather than a live generative model. It collects no names or personal information and sends no learner data elsewhere.

Accessibility and UDL

The lesson supports keyboard navigation, visible focus, responsive text, reduced-motion preferences, high-contrast colors, plain-language definitions, multiple representations, learner choice, scaffolded feedback, and written or printable expression. Optional browser-based read-aloud provides play, pause, resume, and stop controls without autoplay. The narration supplements rather than replaces visible text and screen-reader support. Teachers may also invite oral, visual, or collaborative final responses that address the same objective.
